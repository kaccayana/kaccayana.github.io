:::note[Source]
James D'Alwis, *An Introduction to Kachchāyana's Grammar of the Pāli Language; with an Introduction, Appendix, Notes, &c.*, Colombo: Williams and Norgate, 1863. Public domain by age. Reproduced from a scan of the University of California copy, [available here as a PDF (15 MB)](/reference/alwis.pdf), as a reading edition: the text was recognised from the page images and then proofread against them page by page. The print's spelling and its accents are kept as printed (long vowels carry a grave accent, `Pàli`, `Kachchàyana`, and in places a circumflex; the niggahīta is written `n`), and so are the author's capitals and small capitals; quotation marks are normalised to straight quotes, and the italics of Pāli words in the print are not reproduced, except in the Corrections, where an italic letter marks a lingual or a long vowel and is kept. Footnotes are numbered through the book; the print's symbols (\* † ‡ § ‖ ¶) are replaced by these numbers. Sinhalese characters are shown as `[Sinhalese]`. The printed page numbers are kept as hidden comments of the form `<!-- p. xvii (PDF p. 21) -->`. A word that could not be read is followed by `[?]`.

This page is one part of D'Alwis's book, reproduced as printed. The front matter and the contents are on the [main page](/kaccayana/alwis/).
:::
