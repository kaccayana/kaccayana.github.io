## Aliṅganāma (Nouns without gender: numerals)

> Athāliṅgesu nāmesu tumhamha saddā vuccante.

Now, among the nouns without gender (`aliṅga`), the words `tumha` ("you") and `amha` ("I") are stated.

> Tesaṃ panāliṅgattā tīsu liṅgesu samānarūpaṃ. ‘‘Tumhamha’’ iti ṭhite syā dyuppatti.

But because they have no gender, their form is the same in the three genders. With `tumha` and `amha` in place, `si` and the rest arise.

> ‘‘Savibhattīnaṃ, tumhamhāka’’nti adhikāro.

“`savibhattīnaṃ`” (together with their inflection) and “`tumhamhākaṃ`” (of `tumha` and `amha`) are a governing rule ([`adhikāra`](/introduction#types-of-suttas)).

### 232 (§140): Tvamahaṃ simhi ca (`tvaṃ`, `ahaṃ` for `tumha`, `amha` before `si`; and `tuvaṃ`) :badge[apavada]{variant=success}

:::tip[Kaccāyana]
<LinkCard title="§140 (232): Tvamahaṃ simhi ca" href="/kaccayana/2-namakappa/2-dutiyakanda#m140" description="`tvaṃ`, `ahaṃ` for `tumha`, `amha` before `si`; and `tuvaṃ`" />
:::

:::note[Analysis]

The governing rules ([`adhikāra`](/introduction#types-of-suttas)) “`savibhattīnaṃ`” and “`tumhamhākaṃ`” are those of [245 (§139): Tumhamhākaṃ tayi mayi](/rupasiddhi/2-namakanda/5-alinganama#r245), a rule Buddhappiya states only at the end of the inflection table.

:::

> Sabbesaṃ tumhaamha saddānaṃ savibhattīnaṃ yathākkamaṃ tvaṃahaṃ iccete ādesā honti si mhi vibhattimhi. Ca saddena tumha ssa tuva ñca hoti. Tvaṃ pumā, tvaṃ itthī, tvaṃ napuṃsakaṃ, tuvaṃ satthā vā. Ahaṃ pumā, ahaṃ itthī, ahaṃ napuṃsakaṃ.

When the inflection `si` (①⨀) follows, the entire words `tumha` ("you") and `amha` ("I"), together with their inflection, are replaced by `tvaṃ` and `ahaṃ` respectively; and by the word `ca`, `tumha` also becomes `tuvaṃ`. `tvaṃ pumā` ("you are a man"), `tvaṃ itthī` ("you are a woman"), `tvaṃ napuṃsakaṃ` ("you are a neuter thing"), or `tuvaṃ satthā` ("you are the teacher"); `ahaṃ pumā` ("I am a man"), `ahaṃ itthī` ("I am a woman"), `ahaṃ napuṃsakaṃ` ("I am a neuter thing").

> Bahuvacane ‘‘sabbanāmakārate paṭhamo’’ti e kāro. Tumhe tiṭṭhatha, bhiyyo amhe mahemase.

In the plural, [§164 (200): Sabbanāmakārate paṭhamo](/kaccayana/2-namakappa/3-tatiyakanda#m164) gives the letter `e`: `tumhe tiṭṭhatha` ("you stand"), `bhiyyo amhe mahemase` ("let us honour [him] still more").

:::tip[Derivation]

|     | tumha + si (①⨀)       | rule |
| --- | --------------------- | ---- |
| →   | (tumha + si → tvaṃ)   | 232 (§140) |
| →   | **tvaṃ**              |      |

|     | amha + si (①⨀)        | rule |
| --- | --------------------- | ---- |
| →   | (amha + si → ahaṃ)    | 232 (§140) |
| →   | **ahaṃ**              |      |

|     | tumha + yo (①⨂)       | rule |
| --- | --------------------- | ---- |
| →   | tumha + (yo → e)      | 200 (§164) |
| →   | tumh + (~~a~~ + e)    | 67 (§83)  |
| →   | **tumhe**             |      |

:::

:::tip[Examples]

> `tvameva asi sambuddho, tuvaṃ satthā anuttaro` \
> [(7D 8.2.5: Somanassapaṭilābhakathā)](https://tipitaka2500.github.io/tipitaka/7D/8/8.2/8.2.5#933) \
⚧️①⨀(tumha) 🔼(eva) ▶️🤘⨀(atthi) 🚹①⨀(sambuddha) ⚧️①⨀(tumha) 🚹①⨀(satthu) 🚹①⨀(anuttara) \
you | indeed | are | the Fully Awakened One | you | the teacher | unsurpassed

> `ehi mātali amhepi, bhiyyo bhiyyo mahemase` \
> [(19Vv 1.4.9: Pītavimānavatthu)](https://tipitaka2500.github.io/tipitaka/19Vv/1/1.4/1.4.9#642) \
⏯🤘⨀(eti) 🚹⓪⨀(mātali) ⚧️①⨂(amha) 🔼(api) 🔼(bhiyyo) 🔼(bhiyyo) ⏯👆⨂(maheti) \
come | Mātali | we | too | more | and more | let us honour

:::

> ‘‘Amhassā’’ti vattate.

“`amhassa`” (of `amha`) continues.

### 233 (§121): Mayaṃ yomhi paṭhame (`mayaṃ` before `yo` of the first form) :badge[apavada]{variant=success}

:::tip[Kaccāyana]
<LinkCard title="§121 (233): Mayaṃ yomhi paṭhame" href="/kaccayana/2-namakappa/2-dutiyakanda#m121" description="`mayaṃ` before `yo` of the first form" />
:::

:::note[Analysis]

“`amhassa`” continues from [243 (§120): Amhassa mamaṃ savibhattissa se](/rupasiddhi/2-namakanda/5-alinganama#r243), a rule Buddhappiya states only later.

:::

> Sabbassa amha saddassa savibhattissa mayaṃ ādeso hoti yo mhi paṭhame. Mayaṃ gacchāma.

When the `yo` of the first form (①⨂) follows, the entire word `amha`, together with its inflection, is replaced by `mayaṃ`: `mayaṃ gacchāma` ("we go").

> Ettha ca ekasmimpi gāravabahumānena bahuttasamāropā bahuvacanaṃ hoti.

And here the plural is used even of one person, since plurality is superimposed on them out of respect and esteem.

:::tip[Derivation]

| --- | --------------------- | ---- |
| →   | (amha + yo → mayaṃ)   | 233 (§121) |
| →   | **mayaṃ**             |      |

:::

> ‘‘Aṃmhī’’ti vattate.

“`aṃmhi`” (before `aṃ`) continues.

### 236 (§146): Tumhassa tuvaṃ tvamaṃmhi (`tuvaṃ`, `tvaṃ` for `tumha` before `aṃ`) :badge[apavada]{variant=success}

:::tip[Kaccāyana]

:::

:::note[Analysis]

The “`tumhamhehi`” and “`ākaṃ`” that continue at the end of this rule are the words of [244 (§161): Tumhamhehi namākaṃ](/rupasiddhi/2-namakanda/5-alinganama#r244), a rule Buddhappiya states later on the page.

### 256 (§390): Catūpapadassa lopo tuttarapadādi cassa cu copi navā (`tu` of `catu` elided and its `ca` becomes `cu`, `co`, in some places) :badge[apavada]{variant=success}

:::tip[Kaccāyana]
<LinkCard title="§390 (256): Catūpapadassa lopo tuttarapadādicassa cucopi navā" href="/kaccayana/5-taddhitakappa#m390" description="`tu` of `catu` elided and its `ca` becomes `cu`, `co`, in some places" />

:::note[Analysis]

The “`chassa`” that continues at the end of this rule is the word of Kaccāyana's §374, which Buddhappiya states in the Taddhitakaṇḍa at [408 (§374): Sa chassa vā](/rupasiddhi/5-taddhitakanda#r408).

:::

> Gaṇane dasa ssādimhi ṭhitassa catu iccetassa upapadassa tu saddo lopo hoti, uttarapadādimhi ṭhitassa catū papadassa ca kārassa cu co ādesā honti navā. Cuddasa, coddasa, catuddasa.

In counting, when the word `catu` ("four") stands as the preceding member (`upapada`) before `dasa`, its `tu` is elided, and the letter `ca` of the preceding member `catu`, standing at the head of the following member, is in some places (`navā`) replaced by `cu` or `co`: `cuddasa`, `coddasa`, `catuddasa` ("fourteen").

> Api ggahaṇena anupapadassāpi gaṇane padādica kārassa lopo, cu co honti navā. Yathā – tālīsaṃ, cuttālīsaṃ, cottālīsaṃ, cattālīsaṃ.

By the mention of `api` ("too"), in counting, the letter `ca` at the head of a word is in some places (`navā`) elided or replaced by `cu` or `co` even when the word is not a preceding member. For example, `tālīsaṃ`, `cuttālīsaṃ`, `cottālīsaṃ`, `cattālīsaṃ` ("forty").

> Pañca ca dasa ca, pañcahi vā adhikā dasāti atthe pañcadasa. ‘‘Tesu vuddhī’’tiādinā pañca saddassa dasa vīsatīsu kvaci pannapaṇṇa ādesā. Pannarasa, paṇṇarasa.

By [§404 (370): Tesu vuddhilopāgamavikāraviparītādesā ca](/kaccayana/5-taddhitakappa#m404) and the rest, the word `pañca` is in some places (`kvaci`) replaced by `panna` or `paṇṇa` before `dasa` and `vīsati`: `pannarasa`, `paṇṇarasa`.

in this | chapter | the cases | nineteen | the duties | fourteen

> `dānūpakārā ca tālīsaṃ` \

### 257 (§376): Dase so niccañca (Always `so` for `cha` before `dasa`: `soḷasa`) :badge[apavada]{variant=success}

:::tip[Kaccāyana]
<LinkCard title="§376 (257): Dase so niccañca" href="/kaccayana/5-taddhitakappa#m376" description="Always `so` for `cha` before `dasa`: `soḷasa`" />

When `dasa` follows, the numeral `cha` ("six") always becomes `so`.

“`saṅkhyānaṃ`” (of the numerals) and “`vā`” also continue.

<a name="r258"></a>

### 258 (§379): La darānaṃ (`l` for the `d` and `r` of numerals: `soḷasa`, `cattālīsaṃ`) :badge[apavada]{variant=success}

:::tip[Kaccāyana]
<LinkCard title="§379 (258): La darānaṃ" href="/kaccayana/5-taddhitakappa#m379" description="`l` for the `d` and `r` of numerals: `soḷasa`, `cattālīsaṃ`" />
:::

:::note[Analysis]

The “`vā`” here, which continues at the end of 257, is that of [253 (§383): Dvekaṭṭhānamākāro vā](/rupasiddhi/2-namakanda/5-alinganama#r253); the “`dassa`”, “`ra`” and “`saṅkhyāne`” carried on at the end of this rule are the words of [254 (§381): Ekādito dassa ra saṅkhyāne](/rupasiddhi/2-namakanda/5-alinganama#r254).

:::

> Saṅkhyānaṃ da kārara kārānaṃ la kārādeso hoti vā.

In the numerals, the letters `d` and `r` are optionally replaced by the letter `l`.

> Laḷā namaviseso. Vavatthitavibhāsatthoyaṃ vā saddo, tena ‘‘soḷasa’’ iti niccaṃ, ‘‘teḷasa, terasa, cattālīsaṃ, cattārīsa’’miti vibhāsā, dasa pannarasādīsu na ca hoti.

`l` and `ḷ` are not distinguished. This word `vā` has the sense of a restricted option (`vavatthitavibhāsā`); hence [the replacement] is constant (`nicca`) in `soḷasa` ("sixteen"), optional (`vibhāsā`) in `teḷasa` or `terasa` ("thirteen") and `cattālīsaṃ` or `cattārīsaṃ` ("forty"), and does not occur in `dasa` ("ten"), `pannarasa` ("fifteen") and the like.

> Satta ca dasa ca, sattahi vā adhikā dasāti atthe sattarasa, sattadasa.

In the sense “seven and ten”, or “ten exceeded by seven”: `sattarasa` or `sattadasa` ("seventeen").

:::tip[Derivation]

|     | ti + dasa                     | rule |
| --- | ----------------------------- | ---- |
| →   | (ti → te) + dasa              | 370 (§404) |
| →   | te + (d → r)asa               | 254 (§381) |
| →   | te + (r → ḷ)asa               | 258 (§379) (optional) |
| →   | **terasa**, **teḷasa**        |      |

|     | cattārīsaṃ, by 413 (§389)     | rule |
| --- | ----------------------------- | ---- |
| →   | cattā + (r → l)īsaṃ           | 258 (§379) (optional) |
| →   | **cattārīsaṃ**, **cattālīsaṃ** |     |

:::

:::tip[Examples]

> `atha kho te soḷasa purisā` \
> [(4V 7.2.3: Abhimārapesana)](https://tipitaka2500.github.io/tipitaka/4V/7/7.2/7.2.3#1542) \
🔼(atha kho) ⚧️①⨂(ta) ⚧️①⨂(cha + dasa) 🚹①⨂(purisa) \
then | those | sixteen | men

> `vīsati niruttiyo, cattālīsaṃ ñāṇāni` \
> [(26Ps 2.6.4: Sattabodhisattavāra)](https://tipitaka2500.github.io/tipitaka/26Ps/2/2.6/2.6.4#1602) \
⚧️①⨀(dvi + dasa) 🚺①⨂(nirutti) ⚧️①⨀(catu + dasa) 🚻①⨂(ñāṇa) \
twenty | expressions | forty | knowledges

:::

> Aṭṭha ca dasa ca, aṭṭhahi vā adhikā dasāti atthe aṭṭhadasa itīdha ā tte kate –

In the sense “eight and ten”, or “ten exceeded by eight”: here, in `aṭṭhadasa`, once the change to `ā` has been made —

> ‘‘Vā, dassa, ra, saṅkhyāne’’ti ca vattate.

“`vā`”, “`dassa`” (of `d`), “`ra`” and “`saṅkhyāne`” (in a numeral) also continue.

<a name="r259"></a>

### 259 (§382): Aṭṭhādito ca (And after `aṭṭha` and the like: `aṭṭhārasa`) :badge[apavada]{variant=success}

:::tip[Kaccāyana]
<LinkCard title="§382 (259): Aṭṭhādito ca" href="/kaccayana/5-taddhitakappa#m382" description="And after `aṭṭha` and the like: `aṭṭhārasa`" />
:::

> Aṭṭhā dito ca dasa saddassa da kārassa ra kāro hoti vā saṅkhyāne. Aṭṭhārasa, aṭṭhādasa.

In a numeral, the letter `d` of the word `dasa` optionally becomes the letter `r` after `aṭṭha` ("eight") and the like as well: `aṭṭhārasa` or `aṭṭhādasa` ("eighteen").

:::tip[Derivation]

|     | aṭṭha + dasa                  | rule |
| --- | ----------------------------- | ---- |
| →   | aṭṭh + (a → ā) + dasa         | 253 (§383) |
| →   | aṭṭhā + (d → r)asa            | 259 (§382) (optional) |
| →   | aṭṭhārasa + yo (①⨂)          | 334 (§601), 63 (§55) |
| →   | aṭṭhāras + (a + yo → a)       | 251 (§134) |
| →   | **aṭṭhārasa**, **aṭṭhādasa**  |      |

:::

:::tip[Example]

> `nissaggiyāni aṭṭhārasa` \
> [(5V 10.4: Asādhāraṇādi)](https://tipitaka2500.github.io/tipitaka/5V/10/10.4#1670) \
🚻①⨂(nissaggiya) ⚧️①⨂(aṭṭha + dasa) \
[rules] entailing forfeiture | eighteen

:::

> Aṭṭhādito ti kiṃ? Catuddasa.

By saying `aṭṭhādito` (after `aṭṭha` and the like) we exclude the `d` kept after `catu`: `catuddasa` ("fourteen").

> Kati saddo bahuvacanantova, ‘‘kati yo’’ itīdha

The word `kati` ("how many") takes only plural endings; here, in `kati` + `yo`,

> Niccaṃ yo lopādi, rassattaṃ, kati tiṭṭhanti, kati passasi, katihi katibhi, katinaṃ, katihi katibhi, katinaṃ, katisu.

the elision of `yo` and the rest are obligatory (`nicca`), and the final stays short: `kati tiṭṭhanti` ("how many stand?"), `kati passasi` ("how many do you see?"), `katihi`, `katibhi` (③⨂), `katinaṃ` (④⨂), `katihi`, `katibhi` (⑤⨂), `katinaṃ` (⑥⨂), `katisu` (⑦⨂).

:::tip[Derivation]

|     | kati + yo (①⨂)               | rule |
| --- | ---------------------------- | ---- |
| →   | kati + ~~yo~~                | 146 (§118) |
| →   | **kati**                     |      |

:::

:::tip[Example]

> `kati nu kho amhākaṃ sikkhāpadāni` \
> [(3V 1.42: Sikkhāpadakathā)](https://tipitaka2500.github.io/tipitaka/3V/1/1.42#473) \
⚧️①⨂(kati) 🔼(nu) 🔼(kho) ⚧️⑥⨂(amha) 🚻①⨂(sikkhāpada) \
how many | ? | indeed | our | training rules

:::
